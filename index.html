ABOUT SAIT

Building a community of technology innovators.

Founded in 1995 under the School of Engineering, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), the Division of Information Technology holds the distinct honor of being the very first department in India to offer Information Technology as an undergraduate B.Tech degree.

Operating under CUSAT's autonomous university framework in Kochi, Kerala, SAIT acts as the intellectual bridge uniting student ambition, academic excellence, faculty mentorship, and international industry networks.
